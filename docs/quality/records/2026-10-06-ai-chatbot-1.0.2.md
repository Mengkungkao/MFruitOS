# 2026-10-06 — AI Chatbot 1.0.2: no voice on the Pi Zero, no listening on the Orange Pi

## Problem

After a fresh mFruit OS install, AI Chatbot 1.0.1 from the Fruit Store and
the user's `.env` copied in place:

- Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W: the chatbot listened and answered in text, with no
  voice from the speaker.
- Orange Pi Zero 2W: the chatbot did not listen at all.

## Evidence and causes

| Device | Evidence (app log, `.env` settings without keys) | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| Pi Zero | `TTS_SERVER=piper`, `PIPER_BINARY_PATH=/path/to/piper-binary` (template placeholder); log `spawn /path/to/piper-binary ENOENT`, `No audio data to play` | configuration: Piper is not installed |
| Orange Pi | `ASR_SERVER=opeai`; log `Plugin not found: asr:opeai` | configuration: typo |
| Orange Pi | after a quick press (`Button released too quickly`, `Killing recording process 2615`), sox 2615 still recording 11 min later; every later press `sox FAIL ... snd_pcm_open error: Device or resource busy`; app log 350 KB of sox progress lines | **app bug** (below) |
| Orange Pi | `OPENAI_VOICE_MODEL=tt-1-hd`; OpenAI 404 "The model `tt-1-hd` does not exist" | configuration: typo (would have stopped the voice next) |

The app showed none of the configuration problems on screen.

### Recorder bug (root cause, reproduced)

sox ignores a SIGINT that arrives while it is opening the capture device.
Reproduced on the Orange Pi with the bundled Node.js `spawn` (as the app
does), three tries per delay: SIGINT at 0 ms stops it; at 50, 100, 200, 300
and 500 ms sox keeps recording (still alive 2 s later); at 1000 and 2000 ms
it stops. SIGTERM at 200 ms, or a second SIGINT after 1 s, stops it. The app
sent one SIGINT and forgot the process, which then held the exclusive
`hw:whisplaysound,0` device.

## Fix

- ai-chatbot `364eaa6` (tag `v1.0.2`), `src/device/audio.ts`: a recorder
  stays tracked until it is seen to exit; SIGINT, then SIGTERM after 1 s,
  then SIGKILL; the next recording waits for that exit. sox runs with `-q`
  (no progress lines in the log).
- `src/utils/setup-problems.ts`: an unknown ASR/LLM/TTS server, Piper
  selected but missing, and OpenAI answering a setting with 401/403/404 (or
  400 for the voice) are shown on the idle screen ("Setup problem in .env:
  …"). Passing failures (network, 5xx) and a too-short recording are not.
- The user's `.env` on both boards (backups `.env.backup-20261006` next to
  them): Pi Zero `TTS_SERVER=openai`; Orange Pi `ASR_SERVER=openai`,
  `TTS_SERVER=openai`, `OPENAI_VOICE_MODEL=tts-1-hd`.
- mFruit OS catalogue pin 1.0.2: asset SHA-256
  `72b9af935e2793e6d00e14cafa90ea4d0c22483245e77f21336e7187a315d48c`.

## Device (both boards, 1.0.2 sideloaded, then the user's fixed `.env`)

| Check | Orange Pi Zero 2W (Ubuntu 22.04.5, mFruit OS `1.4.0-local20261005233456`) | Pi Zero 2 W (Debian 13, mFruit OS `1.4.0-local20261005121024`) |
|---|---|---|
| Install 1.0.1 → 1.0.2 (`mfruitctl sideload`) | ok, previous kept | ok in about 65 s, previous kept |
| Real sox, stop at 200 ms then record again, 5 rounds | SIGTERM needed in 5/5; no sox left; next recording got audio | SIGTERM needed in 4/5; no sox left; next recording got audio |
| Loopback: OpenAI voice → speaker → microphone → OpenAI ASR | "Hello, this is the speaker test for your AI chatbot." 10/10 words | same, 10/10 |
| Real OpenAI error, `OPENAI_VOICE_MODEL=tt-1-hd` | HTTP 404 → idle text "Setup problem in .env: OpenAI voice (OPENAI_VOICE_MODEL=tt-1-hd): The model …" | not run |
| Launch → `app.exit.request` | home, no sox/node/UI process left | home, no process left |

The loopback is a physical check: the microphone heard the speaker.
